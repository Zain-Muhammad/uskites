    1.5 Tawa vs Tawa Gudda: which kite should you buy?

    The 1.5 Tawa kite (dedh tawa patang) is the classic Lahori size. It is light, quick and easy to control, which makes it ideal for pecha and for both beginners and experienced flyers. The Tawa Gudda is larger and more stable. It holds steady in stronger winds and climbs high, so many families pick it for relaxed, long flying sessions. Most rooftops order a mix of both.

    Why approved Dor Pinna matters

    Metal wire, nylon and chemically coated (glass) string have caused serious accidents in the past and are banned. Our Dor Pinna uses approved cotton thread only, so you fly within the law and keep your neighbours, motorcyclists and children safe.
